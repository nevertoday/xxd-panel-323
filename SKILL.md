## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one handmade brand mark drawn in a thin white line, not a filled illustration. The line wobbles, varies and misses a little, like chalk or a marker. The ground may carry a tactile material, but the symbol stays a single outline with air around it. Botanical or organic simplification is welcome. Reject a perfect vector logo, a heavy fill, a complex scene and several outlines competing.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-323/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
